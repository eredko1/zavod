# Pillar, pier and elevated-structure sources

The connected sources provide some mapped supports and useful structural constraints. No public citywide dataset locating every elevated train or highway column has been verified. The [source-gap catalog](SOURCE-GAP-CATALOG.md) records coverage and the latest official-source search. Keep observation data in the source merge; geometry reconstructed from points, standard templates, spacing or clearances belongs in rendering and its estimate log.

| Source | Available evidence | Current use and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OSM bridge supports](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:bridge:support) | Explicit mapped pier, abutment or pylon positions/outlines and any recorded tags | Already collected and classified. Eligible mapped pylons use estimated frames. The saved Lower Manhattan 2 km capture has one pylon and one abutment way near Brooklyn Bridge; the saved Coney 2 km capture has none. Missing member fields remain unknown. |

Its bridge point is not a pier; span count does not supply column positions, number of columns per bent, cap shape or foundations. |

| [NYC transport and station layers](INFRASTRUCTURE.md) | Deck/structure outlines and source spot observations | Already collected. These constrain decks and roles; they do not provide a complete support-member inventory. Waterfront pier outlines describe a different object from bridge columns. |

They describe typical designs; project-specific dimensions, elevations and layouts require project plans. They can inform explicit estimated renderer templates, but supply no NYC support coordinates or proof that a particular bridge uses that design. The sheets declare elevations in metres and dimensions in millimetres unless noted. |

The set uses feet/inches and leaves project placement/dimensions to the designer. Keep sheet edition and its own units; these designs still provide no measured NYC column locations. Other drawing groups were not decoded. |
| [NYC 2017 LiDAR via NOAA](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/64728) | Point returns, including classified bridge decks and transit stairwells | [Live original point acquisition](SOURCE-ACQUISITION.md) retains every intersecting EPT depth, lossless compressed point bytes and decoded validation evidence. Exposed support evidence requires actual point inspection; aerial occlusion can hide undersides. |

These add access observations near stations; they do not measure stair geometry or locate structural columns. |
| [Original NYC planimetrics](SOURCE-ACQUISITION.md#original-nyc-planimetrics) | Native transport/rail structure geometry, rail alignments, pavement/curb/wall XYZ and original classification/status fields | Queried live in the original CRS and geographic form. Capture roles can describe roofs or boundaries; source Z is retained without inventing deck grades, wall heights or supports. Related Socrata exports share survey lineage and are not independent confirmation. |

## Evidence and next experiments

Original public responses, both metric and current US Customary pier PDFs, and their extracted text are preserved in ignored `.tmp/map-lab/support-investigation/`; `summary.json` records URLs, acquisition times, byte counts, checksums and complete field lists. The [source audit](SOURCE-FIDELITY-AUDIT.md#pillars-supports-and-elevated-structures) records earlier scoped OSM/NYC observations. NYSDOT inventory captures and tests are documented in [transport inventories](TRANSPORT-INVENTORIES.md).

Pier drawings can be fetched from their public online URLs without a permanent local citywide download. They are PDFs, rather than a verified geographic pier-query API. The retained `online-access.json` records a real PDF GET with no advertised browser CORS permission; a direct browser adapter/proxy has not been added. Station/point-data captures are preserved under `.tmp/map-lab/native-layers/`; original planimetric/entrance captures are preserved separately under `.tmp/map-lab/open-review/captures/`, documented in [source acquisition](SOURCE-ACQUISITION.md).

The next geometry experiment is a bounded LiDAR tile inspection at an elevated road/rail corridor: verify the actual point classes, coordinate references and exposed returns before claiming support fidelity. NOAA's documented stairwell-class remapping is another reason to inspect the delivered LAS headers/classes rather than copy the original survey labels. For surveyed cap/member dimensions and column placement, obtain project-specific plans tied to bridge/station identity and edition; [NYC DOT's records page](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/about/foil.shtml) describes a location-specific request route, not a public citywide feature API. The newly queried [building-grade inventory](SOURCE-ACQUISITION.md#building-grade-and-floor-observations) adds building-adjacent grade and floor references, not supports or roadway profiles. No project-specific plans have been obtained in this review, and no automatic geographic adapter or procedural pillar placement was added from the standard drawings.

Renderer templates can later use these drawings with explicit selected family, estimated dimensions, evidence and clearance checks. All original mapped and inventory alternatives remain available. Source merging must never turn a bridge centroid, route line, station centroid, span count or standard detail into a measured support object.
