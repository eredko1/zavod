# Map Lab source-gap catalog

Review date: September 29, 2026. Scope: the 50 registered live area sources (OSM/Overpass plus 49 entries in [`NYC_SOURCES`](../data/map-sources.js)), the saved Coney and Lower Manhattan 2 km captures, and the official catalogs and capture rules linked below. The first 48 sources were sampled separately across those full-area captures; the overhead-sign and height-restricted-bridge sources have their own later live scoped captures. A gap here means the connected sources have not **verified** that measurement, not that it cannot exist in a project file or a different survey. Source acquisition and merge preserve original observations, provenance, and alternatives; only rendering may reconstruct unmeasured geometry. See the [source/render boundary](SOURCE-RENDER-BOUNDARY.md) and [per-source size record](SOURCE-SIZES.md).

## Connected-source coverage

Each source ID appears in exactly one group below. The grouped IDs distinguish overlapping editions of the same survey from independent evidence. The [fidelity audit](SOURCE-FIDELITY-AUDIT.md) and [acquisition guide](SOURCE-ACQUISITION.md) describe fields and capture methods in more detail.

| Domain | Connected source IDs | Observed data and principal measurement gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OSM physical features and graph | `osm-overpass` | Tagged building/part/support geometry, roads/rails and other physical features. Coverage and dimensional tags vary by mapper; a tag or ordered bridge `layer` is not a surveyed elevation. |
| Buildings and rooftop equipment | `nyc-buildings`, `nyc-building-grade`, `nyc-buildings-2014`, `nyc-water-tanks`, `nyc-cooling-towers-2022` | Footprints/identity/heights, grade/floor references, native 2014 triangles, tank envelopes and cooling outlines. Missing or unverified: complete part/roof semantics, watertank caps/legs, cooling-unit heights, exact under-building clearances and a validated 2014 EGM96-to-current-datum placement. |
| Roads and pedestrian surfaces | `nyc-roadbed`, `nyc-sidewalk`, `nyc-median`, `nyc-curbs`, `nyc-pavement`, `nyc-lion`, `nysdot-ramps`, `nysdot-roadways`, `nysdot-bridge-roadway`, `nyc-pedestrian-ramps`, `nyc-pedestrian-progress`, `nyc-curbs-2022`, `nyc-pavement-2022`, `nyc-curb-cuts-2022`, `nyc-sidewalk-lines-2022`, `nyc-pavement-carto-2022` | Pavement/curb outlines, network identity, ramp/roadway routes and inventory measures, surveyed curb cuts. Missing: continuous 3D ramp/deck grades, crossfall, curb dimensions, per-lane surfaces and measured underside clearances. The sampled NYSDOT ramp/roadway Z channels were zero; LION levels are graph order. |
| Bridges, elevated rail and supports | `nyc-transport`, `nyc-railroad`, `nyc-rail-structures`, `nysdot-bridges`, `nysdot-height-restricted-bridges`, `usdot-nbi-2023`, `nyc-transport-2022`, `nyc-rail-structures-2022`, `nyc-railroad-2022`, `mta-stations`, `mta-entrances` | Bridge/station identity and type, deck/rail/roof outlines with selected XYZ, sparse spot elevations, reported point clearances, span attributes and entrance points. Missing: citywide individual highway/train support locations and sections, continuous deck underside/thickness and road grades, verified station platform and stair geometry. Bridge inventory points and station centroids are *not* pier coordinates. |
| Terrain and point evidence | `nyc-elevation`, `nyc-lidar-2017` | Sparse source elevation classes and complete 2017 LiDAR point records. Missing: validated interpretation of exposed supports/roofs/terrain, hidden undersides, reliable structure-specific class labels and explicit vertical reconciliation across source editions. Raw points are measurements, not already segmented solids. |
| Water, coast and boardwalk | `nyc-boardwalk`, `nyc-shoreline`, `nyc-hydro-structures`, `nyc-hydrography` | Boardwalk/waterfront footprints, shoreline, water/beach categories and some surface levels. Missing: boardwalk posts and deck underside, seawall/pier foundations, bathymetric surface and dated tidal water level. A waterfront `pier` is not a bridge-support pier. |
| Landscape and small structures | `nyc-trees`, `nyc-retaining-walls`, `nyc-retaining-walls-2022`, `nyc-misc-structures-2022`, `nysdot-overhead-signs`, `nyc-parking-2022`, `nyc-plazas-2022`, `nyc-parks-2022`, `nyc-open-space-2022`, `nyc-pools-2022`, `nyc-construction-2022` | Tree DBH/species/condition, wall alignments, sign-asset points, structure and land-use outlines. Missing: tree crowns/heights, measured wall cross-sections, sign faces/posts/gantry spans, many object heights/materials and complete current construction-state geometry. NYC miscellaneous polygons include sign gantries but are not a bridge-column inventory. |

## Support and pillar search

| Candidate | Verification | Decision for merge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OSM mapped `bridge:support`](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:bridge:support) | The saved Lower Manhattan 2 km Overpass response has **two** explicit support ways: `way/1255363983` (`pylon`, 29 nodes, tagged height 82.9 m) and `way/1255363984` (`abutment`, 25 nodes, tagged height 12 m), near the Brooklyn Bridge. The saved Coney 2 km response has **zero** explicit support records. Counts are from raw `elements` and are area-specific. | Already collected as mapped geometry/tags. Keep these exact outlines and identities; do not extend their coverage to unmapped supports or interpret tagged heights as a measured cross-section. |
| [NYC planimetric capture rules](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-planimetrics/blob/main/Capture_Rules.md) | Transport structures are bridge/viaduct/deck polygons; elevated railroad structures are station/platform/roof outlines. Spot bridge elevations are sampled at ends, middle and intervals. The listed feature classes have no dedicated bridge/highway/train column class; waterfront-pier polygons describe shoreline structures. | Already queried in current and original XYZ editions. Keep decks, station roofs and elevation observations under their own roles; no support objects can be declared from these alone. |
| [NYSDOT bridge service](https://gis.dot.ny.gov/hostingny/rest/services/Asset/NYSDOT_Structures/FeatureServer/0), [USDOT NBI](https://geo.dot.gov/server/rest/services/Hosted/National_Bridge_Inventory/FeatureServer/0) | Both connected layers are bridge-point inventories. Their span, material, clearance and pier-protection attributes describe a bridge, not an array of pier coordinates or cap sections. | Already queried live with full records. Use as structural references and identity constraints only. |
| [NYSDOT height-restricted bridges](https://gis.dot.ny.gov/hostingny/rest/services/Geocortex/osowscreen_hoocs/MapServer/2) | Queryable bridge points with carried/crossed roles, inspection dates and reported minimum, posted and permitted clearances. It still has no individual supports or underside surfaces. | Now queried live with complete raw records. Keep zero/high sentinel-like values and original units pending field-dictionary validation; a reported point clearance cannot become a continuous deck/ramp model in source merge. |
| [NYSDOT overhead sign structures](https://gis.dot.ny.gov/hostingny/rest/services/Asset/NYSDOT_Structures/FeatureServer/2) | A public queryable **point** layer with asset/status IDs, without Z, member count, width or support-column geometry. It is a different asset class from bridge piers. | Now connected and queried live alongside NYC miscellaneous polygons, with original native/GeoJSON responses retained as a distinct sign-asset reference. It cannot close the highway/train pillar gap. |
| [2017 NYC LiDAR](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/64728) | Original returns are connected and decodable. The catalog does not label structural columns separately; aerial returns may contain exposed portions but can miss members occluded by decks or roofs. We have not validated a support extraction against ground truth. | Keep measured points with tile/record provenance. A renderer experiment may later extract visible surfaces, labelled as derived candidates rather than surveyed pillar features. |
| [NYSDOT standard pier details](SUPPORT-SOURCES.md) | Public drawings show pier, cap and footing families and typical dimensions but do not assign locations or design variants to a bridge. | Keep as design references outside the geographic source registry; any instantiated members would be renderer estimates. |
| [NYC Municipal Archives bridge plans](https://a860-collectionguides.nyc.gov/repositories/2/accessions/1750), [MTA CADD manual](https://www.mta.info/document/31031), [NYC DOT records route](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/about/foil.shtml) | Project-specific drawings may show pier plans, elevations and existing sections. The public archives index is historical and uneven; no verified citywide spatial support API or complete digital plan set was found in this search. Plans must be checked for edition, bridge identity and as-built versus design status. | Best lead for exact geometry at a named bridge. Acquire and georeference a specific project's records before adding measured support locations. No new live source was registered on the strength of a catalog entry. |

The strongest next bounded check is one named elevated highway or train structure: match its BIN/project identifier to record drawings, and inspect the corresponding LiDAR tiles and ground-level evidence. This tests whether actual columns are recoverable and how many are invisible from the air. The search above covers the official NYC/NYSDOT/MTA/federal sources inspected here; it is not proof that no other provider holds support geometry.

## Priority gaps for source work

| Priority | Missing evidence | Why the current sources do not settle it | Best verified lead / next check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Individual train/highway pillar XY, section, height and foundation | Sparse OSM supports; bridge/station catalogs locate parent structures only; aerial LiDAR can be occluded. | Project-specific as-built plans tied to BIN or MTA structure ID, then field/LiDAR comparison. |
| 2 | Continuous ramp grades, deck crossfall and underside surfaces | NYSDOT route lines and NYC deck boundaries lack complete surface/underside measurements; sampled Z channels were zero. The new state bridge-point layer gives some reported minimum/posted/permitted clearances, but no continuous profile. | Validate the bridge clearance field dictionary, crossing role and dated values; inspect original NYC PolygonZ/spot points and plans against a bounded ramp before renderer interpolation. |
| 3 | Platform, stair and under-building clearance geometry | Station outlines mostly trace roofs/platform extents; building footprints and rooftop meshes do not certify open ground-floor space. | Look for project drawings or actual point/ground observations at a selected site. |
| 4 | Rooftop small equipment shape and complete roof classes | Tank layer supplies envelopes; mesh/point cloud lacks assured component identity, and 2014 versus current dates may conflict. | Inspect captured tank/mesh/LiDAR records together at named buildings; retain conflicts. |
| 5 | Reliable roof/deck/terrain vertical alignment | The 2014 scene declares EGM96 Z, modern NYC layers NAVD88; LiDAR frame and source roles differ. | Verify transformations and feature-specific vertical semantics with control observations before selecting absolute placement. |
| 6 | Bathymetry, boardwalk posts, tree crowns and wall sections | Connected outlines/points do not carry all 3D dimensions. | Search coverage/metadata for local bathymetry and ground surveys; otherwise leave unmeasured parts for explicit renderer estimates. |
| 7 | Sign faces, gantry spans and individual posts | The connected state sign layer gives an asset point/status; NYC miscellaneous polygons give some gantry outlines. Neither establishes post count, section, height or face orientation. | Match a named sign asset to a surveyed drawing or ground observation before a measured sign model is selected. |

The [transport inventory](TRANSPORT-INVENTORIES.md), [support-source review](SUPPORT-SOURCES.md), [fidelity audit](SOURCE-FIDELITY-AUDIT.md) and [size report](SOURCE-SIZES.md) remain the detailed evidence for each connected source. This catalog records *missing measurements* and candidate ways to verify them; it does not promote a catalog hit, interpolation or standard drawing to measured merge geometry.
