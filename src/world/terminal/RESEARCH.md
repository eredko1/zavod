# TERMINAL — Grand Central Terminal research → build plan

Coordinate frame: metres, origin = centre of the Main Concourse floor. **East = +x, North = −z, up = +y.**
42nd Street is to the south (+z), Vanderbilt Ave west (−x), Lexington Ave east (+x).

Light: south-easterly sun (mid-morning) — the famous shafts fall from the south clerestory and east windows across the floor toward the west. Warm interior (2700 K bulbs) vs cool blue-green daylight glass; lower levels warm incandescent; subway cool fluorescent.

## Nav/engine constraint that shapes the plan
`groundHeight(x,z)` is a single value per (x,z) and is the hard floor for the player and the AI's base floor. Overlapping playable levels therefore cannot both host AI. Decision: the AI's floors are the Main Concourse (0), the balconies (+6, groundHeight=6 in their footprint; the arcade beneath is closed with bronze grilles), the ramps/gallery/dining level (−6) and the subway (−12). Vanderbilt Hall (0) sits over the Dining Concourse, so it is a collider-slab floor: player-reachable, AI does not path there. The real Dining Concourse lies under the Main Concourse; here it is shifted south under Vanderbilt Hall so the concourse floor stays the AI's base.
